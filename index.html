01 / ABOUT

Designing systems players can discover.

I'm Logan Ancell, a game development student and independent developer interested in the intersection between gameplay programming and game design.

I enjoy developing systems that give players something to discover, understand, and interact with rather than simply placing mechanics in front of them.

Much of my current work focuses on creature gameplay, progression, worldbuilding, player research systems, and building playable prototypes that allow ideas to be tested and improved through iteration.
